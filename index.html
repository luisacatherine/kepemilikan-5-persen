Kepemilikan 5%Daily shareholder changes from KSEI SID ownership reports

→

Reading the data. KSEI publishes holders at or above 5% of an emiten, by SID, broken down across the securities accounts they hold through. A holder tagged crossed 5% may have held a smaller stake the day before, and one tagged below 5% may still hold shares. Click any row to see which custodian actually moved — a stake can stay the same size and still shift between brokers, which this tags moved broker.

Broker codes. The two-letter code is IDX's identifier for an exchange member, the same one you see in a broker summary. It only exists for exchange members: a stake held through a custodian bank such as Deutsche Bank or Standard Chartered shows bank, and one parked in a KSEI holding account for a broker whose membership was revoked shows closed. Codes are matched from brokers.csv; anything the build cannot match is listed when it runs, rather than quietly dropped.

Why this rebuilds the comparison. Each KSEI file already contains a before and after column, but those columns are unreliable across a record change. When KSEI merged two duplicate Kimia Farma records in PEHA on 2 Sep, its own file reported a jump from 238,450,930 to 476,901,860 — an apparent doubling of a 28% stake. Kimia Farma had in fact held 56.77% on both days through two SIDs. This page therefore reconstructs a snapshot per date, folds respellings of one holder together, and compares the snapshots itself.
